# Summary

- Scrum runs on a work, inspect and adapt loop, which is why sprints are capped at one month.
- Roles, events and artifacts: 3 roles, 5 events, and 3 artifacts with their commitments. Review inspects the product with stakeholders, and the Retro inspects how the Scrum Team works, without stakeholders.
Kanban is continuous flow on a board. WIP limits expose bottlenecks and force finishing before starting.
- Forecasting: give a range, “likely X, up to Y, assuming Z”. Use average velocity for the likely case and the lowest recent sprint for the worst case.
- The DoD is one quality checklist owned by the whole Scrum Team. Items not Done earn no velocity.

## 3 quick tips

- Read the Scrum Guide sections on Developers, the Increment and the DoD once before AGL-02.
- In any forecast, state a range and the assumptions. Keep “no risk-free” in mind.
- Count elapsed time between timestamps. Don’t count both end days.
